How Retrivo Works in 3 Steps

A transparent, human-verified protocol designed to protect student belongings from opportunistic theft.

01

Report with Attributes

Log your lost or found item with exact category, colour, campus facility, date, and a secret identifying mark for ownership verification.

02

5-Point Match Engine

Our backend matching engine scores report pairs out of 100 points based on category (+30), colour (+20), location (+20), date (+16), and keywords (+14).

03

Verify & Recover

The claimant proves ownership through secret details. Once verified by the finder or clerk, coordinate a safe handover on campus.

No black box. Five attributes. Maximum 100 points.
