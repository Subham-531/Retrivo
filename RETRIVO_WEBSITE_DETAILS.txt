1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Title   : Retrivo: Smart Lost & Found Matcher for College Campuses
Version         : 2.0 (University Property Office Counter Edition)
Academic Domain : Database Management Systems (DBMS) Full-Stack Laboratory Project
Core Problem    : On university campuses, thousands of valuable personal items
                  (chargers, student IDs, keys, headphones, calculators, flasks)
                  are misplaced each semester. Traditional physical lost-and-found
                  desks are unsearchable, fragmented across departments, and expose
                  belongings to opportunistic theft.
Solution        : A unified digital recovery platform with a transparent, rule-based
                  5-point compatibility scoring algorithm, encrypted ownership
                  verification, 3NF relational database schema, ACID transaction
                  recovery logging, and an interactive admin moderation desk.

Visual Metaphor : 1970s University Property Office Counter — the lost-and-found
                  bureau in the student union basement with a ledger register, brass
                  bell, rubber date stamps, and wooden card catalog drawers.

3. DATABASE ARCHITECTURE & DBMS VIVA CONCEPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The database ('retrivo_db') is fully normalized up to Third Normal Form (3NF),
enforcing referential integrity, constraints, and audit logging.

3.1 Relational Tables (7 Core Entities):
  1. users:
     - Stores student and administrator accounts.
     - Columns: id (PK), full_name, email (UNIQUE), password_hash (BCrypt),
       role (ENUM: 'STUDENT', 'ADMIN'), phone, created_at.
     - Constraints: CHECK (email LIKE '%@%._%').

  2. categories:
     - Master taxonomy for categorizing campus belongings.
     - Columns: id (PK), name (UNIQUE), description, icon.
     - 8 Pre-seeded Categories: Electronics & Gadgets, Bags & Wallets,
       Books & Stationery, Keys & Access Cards, Clothing & Accessories,
       ID Cards & Documents, Water Bottles & Flasks, Other Personal Items.

  3. items:
     - Stores LOST and FOUND reports submitted by campus members.
     - Columns: id (PK), user_id (FK -> users.id), category_id (FK -> categories.id),
       item_name, colour, brand, description, location, event_date,
       report_type (ENUM: 'LOST', 'FOUND'), status (ENUM: 'ACTIVE',
       'CLAIM_PENDING', 'RETURNED', 'CLOSED'), image_path, identifying_details,
       created_at, updated_at.
     - Privacy Rule: 'identifying_details' stores private ownership marks and is
       never displayed publicly.

  4. matches:
     - Stores calculated compatibility between compatible report pairs (LOST vs FOUND).
     - Columns: id (PK), lost_item_id (FK -> items.id), found_item_id (FK -> items.id),
       compatibility_score (INT, 0-100), matching_reasons (JSON),
       status (ENUM: 'SUGGESTED', 'NOTIFIED', 'CLAIMED', 'RESOLVED', 'DISMISSED'),
       created_at.
     - Constraints: UNIQUE(lost_item_id, found_item_id),
       CHECK(lost_item_id <> found_item_id).

  5. claims:
     - Stores claimant ownership proofs and verification decisions.
     - Columns: id (PK), match_id (FK -> matches.id), claimant_id (FK -> users.id),
       found_item_id (FK -> items.id), proof_message (TEXT),
       status (ENUM: 'PENDING', 'APPROVED', 'REJECTED', 'COMPLETED'),
       reviewed_by (FK -> users.id), review_notes, created_at, reviewed_at.

  6. claim_messages:
     - Real-time direct communication between claimant and finder to coordinate proof and pickup.
     - Columns: id (PK), claim_id (FK -> claims.id ON DELETE CASCADE),
       sender_id (FK -> users.id ON DELETE CASCADE), content (TEXT),
       is_read (BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE), created_at (TIMESTAMP).
     - Constraints: INDEX(claim_id), INDEX(created_at).
     - Security: Restricted strictly to the claimant, finder, lost-item owner, or Role.ADMIN.

  7. notifications:
     - In-app notification center for automated match alerts, claim decisions, and new chat messages.
     - Columns: id (PK), user_id (FK -> users.id), title, message,
       type (ENUM: 'MATCH_ALERT', 'CLAIM_SUBMITTED', 'CLAIM_APPROVED',
       'CLAIM_REJECTED', 'HANDOVER_COMPLETED'), is_read (BOOLEAN), created_at.

  8. item_history:
     - Comprehensive audit trail of item lifecycle status changes.
     - Columns: id (PK), item_id (FK -> items.id), old_status, new_status,
       changed_by_user_id (FK -> users.id), notes, changed_at.

3.2 Relational Views (4 Pre-Compiled Views):
  - vw_active_reports:
    Active lost and found items joined with category and user contact info.
  - vw_high_confidence_matches:
    Match pairs with score >= 80% with lost and found item titles and reporters.
  - vw_category_loss_statistics:
    Total reports, recovered count, and recovery rate percentage grouped by category.
  - vw_pending_claims_detail:
    Pending claims with claimant and finder contact info and item locations.

3.3 ACID Transactions & Decoupled Claim/Handover Lifecycle:
  - Two-Stage Verification & Handover Protocol:
    1. Claim Submission:
       - Claimant submits proof of ownership against a matched found report.
       - Claim created with status = 'PENDING'.
       - Items transitioned to status = 'CLAIM_PENDING'. Match set to 'CLAIMED'.
       - Notification dispatched to finder.
    2. Claim Approval (Verification Stage):
       - Finder or Administrator verifies claimant's statement.
       - Claim status updated to 'APPROVED' with reviewed_at timestamp.
       - Crucial Property Office Rule: Items remain 'CLAIM_PENDING' (NOT prematurely RETURNED).
       - Claimant is notified to coordinate physical recovery at the campus Property Office.
    3. Physical Handover Confirmation (Recovery Stage):
       - Confirmed by finder, claimant, or administrator via /api/claims/{id}/complete.
       - Claim status becomes 'COMPLETED'.
       - Both lost and found items transitioned atomically to 'RETURNED'.
       - Match transitioned to 'RESOLVED'.
       - Any competing pending claims on the same found item are automatically rejected.
       - Item history records generated and in-app notifications dispatched to both parties.
       - All changes executed in a single atomic ACID transaction.


4. SMART 5-POINT RULE-BASED MATCHING ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The matching algorithm in 'MatchingService.java' evaluates compatible report pairs
(LOST vs FOUND) across 5 weighted criteria for a maximum score of 100 points:

  1. Category Match (30 Points Max):
     - Exact Match (30 pts): lost.category_id = found.category_id.
     - No Match (0 pts): Reports with different categories are incompatible.

  2. Colour Match (20 Points Max):
     - Exact Match (20 pts): Primary colour names match case-insensitively.
     - Partial Overlap (12 pts): One colour string contains the other (e.g. "Dark Blue" vs "Blue").

  3. Location / Campus Facility Proximity (20 Points Max):
     - Exact Match (20 pts): Reported at identical location (e.g. "Library 2nd Floor").
     - Facility Proximity Zone (16 pts): Items reported within the same campus
       zone (e.g. both in "Library", "CS Computer Lab", "Main Cafeteria", "Sports Complex").

  4. Date Proximity Timeline (16 Points Max):
     - Same Day (16 pts): Found on the exact date lost.
     - Within 24 Hours (14 pts): Found 1 day after loss.
     - Within 48 Hours (12 pts): Found 2 days after loss.
     - Within 5 Days (8 pts): Found within 5 days.
     - Within 10 Days (4 pts): Found within 10 days.
     - Negative Timeline: If found date is before lost date by >2 days, penalized to 0 pts.

  5. Title & Description Keyword Overlap (14 Points Max):
     - Brand Match (+6 pts): Identical brand (e.g. "Apple", "HP", "Milton", "Casio").
     - Tokenized Jaccard Similarity (+8 pts): Token overlap of normalized keywords.

Compatibility Tiers:
  - Strong Match (>= 80 Points) : Automatically triggers in-app notification alerts to both students.
  - Possible Match (50 - 79 Points) : Recommended in student portal smart match feed.
  - Low Match (< 50 Points) : Filtered out to prevent notification noise.


5. USER ROLES & ACCESS CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Retrivo implements Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) via Spring Security:

5.1 Student Role ('ROLE_STUDENT'):
  - File LOST and FOUND reports with public descriptions and private verification details.
  - Search and filter campus reports in the card catalog.
  - Access personal Student Ledger (active reports, match suggestions, submitted claims).
  - File ownership claims on matched found items with proof statements.
  - Approve or reject claims submitted on items they found.
  - Receive real-time notification alerts.

5.2 Admin Role ('ROLE_ADMIN'):
  - Access the Property Office Admin Moderation Desk.
  - View real-time campus KPI analytics (users, reports, recoveries, calculated matches, recovery rate).
  - Interactive Chart.js analytics: monthly reporting vs. recovery trends & category distribution doughnut.
  - Content moderation: override status of any campus report (ACTIVE, CLAIM_PENDING, RETURNED, CLOSED).
  - Administrative deletion of duplicate, spam, or inappropriate reports.
  - Directory oversight of all registered campus users.

6.2 Typography Tokens:
  - Display Headings : "Fraunces", high-contrast serif with optical sizing.

  - Numbers & IDs    : "JetBrains Mono", fixed-width numerals for report IDs, scores, dates.
  - UI Labels & CTAs : "Inter", clean sans-serif for buttons, inputs, and navigation.

6.3 Design Rules & Non-Negotiables:
  - Zero Blue / Purple : No indigo, blue, violet, or AI sunset gradients anywhere.
  - Zero Emojis        : Replaced with line-drawn SVG Lucide icons (MapPin, Calendar, Bell, Stamp, ShieldCheck).
  - Strict 4px Scale   : Every padding, margin, and gap is a multiple of 4 (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64px).
  - Rubber Stamps      : Status badges render as rotated ink-stamp impressions (-2.5deg / +2deg) with dashed borders.
  - Tactile Depth      : Buttons feature physical press states (translateY +1px, inset shadow).

8. REST API SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Base URL: http://localhost:8080/api

Authentication Endpoints:
  POST   /api/auth/login                  - Authenticate user, returns JWT token & role
  POST   /api/auth/register               - Register new student account

Item & Catalog Endpoints:
  GET    /api/items/browse                - Filter & search catalog (query, category, colour, status, page, size)
  GET    /api/items/{id}                  - Retrieve detailed item report
  GET    /api/items/recent                - Retrieve 6 most recent active campus reports
  GET    /api/items/my-items              - Retrieve reports submitted by authenticated user
  POST   /api/items                       - Submit new lost or found report (requires auth)
  POST   /api/items/upload-image          - Upload item photograph (multipart, max 5MB)
  DELETE /api/items/{id}                  - Delete own report

Smart Match Endpoints:
  GET    /api/matches                     - Retrieve algorithmic matches relevant to authenticated user
  GET    /api/matches/item/{itemId}       - Retrieve matches for a specific item report
  GET    /api/matches/all                 - Retrieve all system matches above threshold

Claims Endpoints:
  POST   /api/claims                      - Submit ownership claim with proof statement
  GET    /api/claims/my                   - Retrieve claims relevant to authenticated user
  GET    /api/claims/{id}                 - Retrieve claim details (claimant, finder, or admin)
  GET    /api/claims/{id}/messages        - Retrieve direct chat history between claimant & finder
  POST   /api/claims/{id}/messages        - Send real-time chat message to counterpart
  PUT    /api/claims/{id}/review          - Approve or reject a claim (finder / admin)
  PUT    /api/claims/{id}/complete        - Confirm physical belongings handover

Category Endpoints:
  GET    /api/categories                  - Retrieve master category taxonomy list

Admin Endpoints (Requires ROLE_ADMIN):
  GET    /api/admin/stats                 - Retrieve campus KPIs, category distribution & trends
  GET    /api/admin/items                 - Retrieve all items across campus for moderation
  GET    /api/admin/users                 - Retrieve registered campus student directory
  PATCH  /api/admin/items/{id}/status     - Override status of any campus report
  DELETE /api/admin/items/{id}            - Permanently delete a report

Notification Endpoints:
  GET    /api/notifications               - Retrieve in-app notifications
  GET    /api/notifications/unread-count  - Retrieve unread notification badge count
  PATCH  /api/notifications/mark-all-read - Mark all alerts as read

test suite located under 'backend/src/test/java/edu/campus/retrivo/service/':

10.1 MatchingServiceTest (5 Unit Tests):
  - testExactMatchAllCriteria:
    Verifies that a 100% identical report pair achieves the maximum 100 points
    with all 5 breakdown reasons generated.
  - testCategoryMismatch:
    Confirms that items in mismatched categories receive 0 category points and
    generate an explicit mismatch reason without producing false matches.
  - testDateProximityDegradation:
    Verifies timeline scoring progression (same day = 16 pts > 1 day = 14 pts >
    4 days = 8 pts > 15 days = 0 pts).
  - testFoundBeforeLostDate:
    Enforces the physical constraint that an item found days before it was lost
    is awarded 0 date proximity points.
  - testCampusZoneMatching:
    Verifies facility zone matching (e.g., Library reading room vs. basement)
    receives 16 points for proximity.

10.2 ClaimServiceTest (7 Unit Tests):
  - testCreateClaimSuccess:
    Verifies claim submission transitions item status to CLAIM_PENDING, match to
    CLAIMED, and creates notifications.
  - testFinderCannotClaimOwnItem:
    Ensures a finder cannot submit a claim against their own found report.
  - testReviewClaimApprovalDecoupledFromReturn:
    CRITICAL: Confirms that claim approval transitions claim to APPROVED while
    items strictly remain CLAIM_PENDING (awaiting physical handover).
  - testUnauthorizedClaimReview:
    Verifies that third-party students cannot approve or reject claims.
  - testMarkCompletedSuccess:
    Verifies physical handover completion transitions claim to COMPLETED, items

  - testMarkCompletedAutoRejectsCompetingClaims:
